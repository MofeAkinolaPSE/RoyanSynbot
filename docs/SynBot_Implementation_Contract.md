NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:


1.   SCOPE OF SERVICES

The Service Provider shall implement, configure, and deploy the SynBot platform - a proprietary,
modular AI-powered business intelligence and operational management system - for the Client. The
implementation shall deliver eleven (11) fully functional modules as set out in Schedule A,
including an AI-powered internal chat assistant, customer-facing website chat widget, live KPI
executive dashboard, inventory intelligence, financial analytics, HR analytics, operations analytics,
CRM and lead management, workflow and audit engine, ERP data integration pipeline, and a real-time
notifications layer. The Platform shall be deployed entirely on the Client's own local server
infrastructure. No recurring cloud hosting fees apply under this Agreement.


2.   FEES AND PAYMENT

2.1  The total implementation fee is ONE MILLION, TWO HUNDRED THOUSAND NAIRA (N1,200,000),
     payable in two equal instalments:

          Initial Implementation Fee:   N600,000   -   due upon execution of this Agreement.
          Final Implementation Fee:     N600,000   -   due upon successful Go-Live and Client sign-off.

2.2  The Initial Implementation Fee authorises the Service Provider to commence all development and
     configuration activities. It must be received within five (5) business days of contract
     execution. The Service Provider reserves the right to defer commencement until payment is
     confirmed.

2.3  The Final Implementation Fee must be settled within seven (7) business days of the Client's
     formal Go-Live sign-off.

2.4  The Initial Implementation Fee is non-refundable once development has commenced, except in the
     event of material breach by the Service Provider. Overdue amounts shall attract interest at
     five percent (5%) per annum from the due date.


3.   PROJECT TIMELINE

The Service Provider shall deliver the complete platform within eight (8) weeks of receipt of the
Initial Implementation Fee, subject to timely cooperation and data access from the Client. Any
material scope changes require a written change order signed by both Parties.


4.   INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

The SynBot platform and all associated intellectual assets remain the exclusive property of the
Service Provider. Upon full payment of the total implementation fee, the Client is granted a
perpetual, non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the Platform solely within its internal
business operations. The Client's business data remains the exclusive property of the Client at all
times. The Client may not sublicense, resell, reverse-engineer, or redistribute the Platform.


5.   CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION

Each Party shall hold all Confidential Information of the other Party in strict confidence and shall
not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent. This obligation survives
termination of this Agreement for five (5) years. Both Parties shall comply with the Nigeria Data
Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 in the performance of this Agreement.


6.   WARRANTIES

The Service Provider warrants that all services shall be performed diligently and in accordance
with industry standards. A thirty (30) day post-Go-Live warranty covers confirmed bugs and
functional defects at no additional cost. The warranty does not cover defects arising from Client
misuse or unauthorised modifications to the Platform.


7.   LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

The Service Provider's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total
implementation fees actually paid by the Client. Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect,
consequential, or special damages, including loss of revenue or loss of data.


8.   TERMINATION

Either Party may terminate this Agreement by fourteen (14) days' written notice upon material
breach that remains unremedied within that period. If the Client terminates after development has
commenced, the Initial Implementation Fee remains non-refundable. If the Service Provider
terminates without cause, all fees paid shall be refunded in full within fourteen (14) days.


9.   GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Any dispute shall first
be subject to good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within twenty-one (21) days of written notice,
the matter shall be referred to binding arbitration in Lagos, Nigeria, under the Arbitration and
Mediation Act 2023, before a single arbitrator appointed by the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators
(Nigeria Branch).


10.  GENERAL

This Agreement together with Schedule A constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and
supersedes all prior discussions and representations. No amendment is valid unless in writing and
signed by both Parties. Neither Party may assign its rights without the prior written consent of
the other. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, or employment
relationship between the Parties.

SCHEDULE A - PLATFORM DELIVERABLES SUMMARY

The following modules shall be delivered and formally accepted by the Client at Go-Live:

Module 1     AI-Powered Internal Chat Assistant (RAG pipeline, role-based modes, EOS briefings)
Module 2     Customer-Facing Website Chat Widget (knowledge base ingestion, lead capture)
Module 3     Live KPI and Executive Dashboard (AR/AP, cashflow, inventory, anomaly detection)
Module 4     Inventory Intelligence and Risk Management (live stock, expiry tiers, IoT integration)
Module 5     Financial Analytics and Reporting (AR aging, GL, margin drivers, forecasting)
Module 6     HR Analytics (headcount, payroll, absences, staff registry)
Module 7     Operations Analytics and Forecasting (fulfilment KPIs, order tracking, webhook)
Module 8     CRM and Lead Management (pipeline, scoring, 360-degree customer view)
Module 9     Workflow, Approvals and Audit Engine (RBAC, JWT authentication, full audit log)
Module 10    ERP Data Integration and Import Pipeline (9 datasets, quality gates, deduplication)
Module 11    Real-Time Notifications and WebSocket Layer (9 broadcast channels, KPI watchdog)

Infrastructure: PostgreSQL and pgvector on Client local server; Docker; Nginx reverse proxy;
                environment security hardening.

Handover:       User guides per module; technical architecture documentation; admin operations
                manual; minimum two (2) staff training sessions; formal Go-Live sign-off.
