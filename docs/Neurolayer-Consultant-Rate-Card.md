## Revenue Allocation Model

On every client payment received, funds are allocated in this order before any distribution:

| Bucket | Allocation | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Consultant Costs | ~20% | Pay the team per deliverables below |
| Operations | 5% | Tools, API costs, licences, equipment |
| Business Reserve | 10% | Float fund — covers 1 month of costs |
| Growth / Reinvestment | 10% | Legal filings, marketing, tooling, development |
| Founder's Profit | ~55% | Moe's draw after all above are funded |

**Payment Timing Rule:** All consultant payments are made within **5 business days of Neurolayer receiving the corresponding client payment**. Milestone-linked — never before.

**WHT Policy:** Withholding Tax (WHT) at 5% is deducted from each consultant payment and remitted to FIRS. Rates below are gross amounts; net payment = rate × 95%.
