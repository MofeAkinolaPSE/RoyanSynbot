---

## NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

---

## 1. ENGAGEMENT

1.1 The Company engages the Consultant as an independent Marketing Analyst on a non-exclusive, per-deliverable basis. The Consultant shall provide marketing and business development services as requested from time to time in accordance with this Agreement.

1.2 This Agreement does not constitute a contract of employment, partnership, or joint venture. The Consultant is an independent contractor and is solely responsible for their own tax affairs, except as expressly provided in clause 3.3.

---

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES & RATE CARD

2.1 The Consultant shall provide services from the following schedule:

| Service | Gross Fee |
|---|---|
| Client acquisition bonus — signed client contract valued at less than ₦2,000,000 | ₦75,000 |
| Client acquisition bonus — signed client contract valued at ₦2,000,000–₦5,000,000 | ₦150,000 |
| Client acquisition bonus — signed client contract valued above ₦5,000,000 | ₦200,000 |
| Pitch deck / proposal per RFP or prospect | ₦40,000 |
| Monthly market intelligence report | ₦30,000/month |
| Monthly campaign execution (lead generation, outreach, content creation) | ₦50,000/month |

2.2 "**Client acquisition**" for the purposes of the bonus in clause 2.1 means: the Consultant identified the prospect, initiated or materially contributed to the outreach, and the prospect subsequently executed a signed Services Agreement with the Company. Introductions made by the Company independently do not qualify for the bonus.

2.3 Work is requested in writing. The Consultant confirms acceptance and timeline within two (2) business days.

---

## 3. PAYMENT TERMS

3.1 **Acquisition bonus:** Paid within five (5) business days of the Company receiving the client's Mobilisation Fee under the signed client contract.

3.2 **Pitch deck / proposal:** Paid within ten (10) business days of delivery, regardless of whether the prospect converts to a client.

3.3 **Monthly services (intelligence report, campaign):** Paid at the end of each calendar month, funded from the Company's retainer revenue. Subject to availability of retainer funds — the Company shall notify the Consultant in advance if monthly services cannot be funded in a given month.

3.4 **Withholding Tax:** The Company shall deduct WHT at **five percent (5%)** from each payment and remit to FIRS. Net payment = gross fee × 95%. The Company shall provide WHT credit notes for the Consultant's tax records.

3.5 All payments are made by bank transfer. No cash payments.

---

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 All work product created by the Consultant in the course of this engagement — including pitch decks, proposals, market reports, campaign assets, content, brand materials, and client research — is the **sole property of the Company** upon creation.

4.2 The Consultant irrevocably assigns to the Company all intellectual property rights in such work product, including copyright. This assignment is effective retroactively to cover all work performed prior to the execution of this Agreement.

---

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 The Consultant shall hold all Confidential Information of the Company and its clients in strict confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent. This obligation survives termination for **five (5) years**.

5.2 "**Confidential Information**" includes prospect lists, client identities and commercial terms, pricing structures, product roadmaps, business strategy, and financial data.

---

## 6. NON-SOLICITATION

6.1 During the term of this Agreement and for **twelve (12) months** following its termination, the Consultant shall not directly solicit, contract with, or provide marketing services to any client of the Company that was introduced to the Consultant through the Company's engagements, without the prior written consent of the Company.

6.2 The Consultant shall not, during the term of this Agreement, provide marketing or business development services to any direct competitor of the Company (i.e., any company building or selling AI-powered hospital or pharmaceutical management software in Nigeria) without prior written disclosure and approval.

---

## 7. NO EQUITY OR PROFIT SHARE

7.1 This Agreement does not create any equity interest, profit-sharing arrangement, or ownership stake in the Company for the Consultant. The Consultant's sole entitlement is the service fees set out in clause 2.1.

---

## 8. TERMINATION & HANDOVER

8.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by **thirty (30) days' written notice**.

8.2 Upon termination, the Consultant shall: (a) deliver all in-progress work product within ten (10) business days; (b) transfer all prospect data, campaign accounts, CRM access, and brand assets to Company-designated accounts; (c) return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Company.

8.3 Acquisition bonuses for prospects who were actively in negotiation at the time of termination and subsequently sign within sixty (60) days of termination shall remain payable to the Consultant at the applicable rate.

---

## 9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

9.2 Disputes shall first be subject to good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within fourteen (14) days, the matter shall be referred to a single arbitrator under the **Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023**, in Lagos, Nigeria.

---

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter.

10.2 No amendment is valid unless made in writing and signed by both Parties.

10.3 The Consultant may not subcontract obligations under this Agreement without prior written consent.
