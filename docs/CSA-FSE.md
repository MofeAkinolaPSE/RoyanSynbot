## NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

---

## 1. ENGAGEMENT

1.1 The Company engages the Consultant as an independent Full Stack Engineer on a non-exclusive, per-deliverable basis. The Consultant shall provide software engineering services to the Company as requested from time to time in accordance with this Agreement.

1.2 This Agreement does not constitute a contract of employment, partnership, or joint venture. The Consultant is an independent contractor and is solely responsible for their own tax affairs, except as expressly provided in clause 3.4.

---

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES & RATE CARD

2.1 The Consultant shall provide engineering services from the following schedule:

| Service | Gross Fee | Scope Definition |
|---|---|---|
| New client — platform deployment, configuration & customisation | ₦350,000 | Docker deployment on client server, environment configuration (.env), database migrations, nginx/TLS setup, and Go-Live handover. Does not include custom feature development, third-party integrations beyond agreed scope, or training beyond the Go-Live session. |
| Data migration / DAL execution (per client) | ₦100,000 | Extract-transform-load pipeline from legacy system to RoyanHealth database. Includes schema discovery, field mapping, bulk load, and spot-check verification. |
| Monthly maintenance — active retainer client | ₦80,000/month | Scheduled maintenance window, security patches, database optimisation, backup restore testing, server health monitoring, and P1–P3 issue resolution per SLA. |
| Feature sprint (agreed scope, 1–2 weeks) | ₦60,000/sprint | Development of a scoped feature set agreed in writing before the sprint begins. Scope change mid-sprint requires a new written agreement. |
| Emergency hotfix — P1 outside maintenance window | ₦30,000/instance | Critical system restoration outside the normal maintenance scope. |

2.2 Work is requested by the Company in writing with a clear scope statement. The Consultant confirms acceptance and timeline within two (2) business days.

2.3 Scope not covered by the above schedule shall be quoted separately before work commences. The Consultant shall not perform out-of-scope work without written approval.

---

## 3. PAYMENT TERMS

3.1 The Consultant shall submit a written invoice to the Company upon completion of each deliverable (or at month-end for monthly maintenance).

3.2 **New client project payments** are milestone-linked:
- **40%** (₦140,000) paid within 5 business days of Company receiving the client's Mobilisation Fee
- **40%** (₦140,000) paid within 5 business days of Company receiving the client's Milestone Fee
- **20%** (₦70,000) paid within 5 business days of Company receiving the client's Go-Live Fee

3.3 **Monthly maintenance fees** are paid within **five (5) business days** of the Company receiving the client's retainer invoice settlement for the corresponding month.

3.4 **Withholding Tax:** The Company shall deduct WHT at **five percent (5%)** from each payment and remit to FIRS. Net payment = gross fee × 95%. The Company shall provide WHT credit notes for the Consultant's tax records.

3.5 All payments are made by bank transfer. No cash payments.

4.2 Upon termination, all Company-paid tool licences must be transferred to Company admin accounts within five (5) business days.

---

## 5. ACCESS & CREDENTIALS

5.1 The Consultant shall ensure the Company (specifically Moe Akinola) has **admin-level access** to all repositories, deployment servers, cloud accounts, and tools used in the course of the engagement at all times. The Consultant shall never be the sole key holder to any system, credential, or resource belonging to the Company or its clients.

5.2 All source code shall be committed to Company-controlled GitHub repositories. No code shall be stored solely on the Consultant's personal devices or private repositories.

---

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 All work product created by the Consultant in the course of this engagement — including source code, scripts, database schemas, configuration files, technical documentation, and all derivative works — is the **sole property of the Company** upon creation.

6.2 The Consultant irrevocably assigns to the Company all intellectual property rights in such work product, including copyright and all related rights. This assignment is effective retroactively to cover all work performed prior to the execution of this Agreement.

6.3 The Consultant warrants that all work product is original, free of third-party claims, and does not incorporate open-source code under licences incompatible with commercial use without prior written disclosure to the Company.

---

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 The Consultant shall hold all Confidential Information of the Company and its clients in strict confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent. This obligation survives termination for **five (5) years**.

7.2 "**Confidential Information**" includes source code, client identities, patient data, clinical records, system architecture, API keys, credentials, and business strategy.

7.3 The Consultant acknowledges that client systems may contain patient health data. The Consultant shall comply with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023** at all times.

---

## 8. NON-SOLICITATION

8.1 During the term of this Agreement and for **twelve (12) months** following its termination, the Consultant shall not directly solicit, contract with, or provide services to any client of the Company that was introduced to the Consultant through the Company's engagements, without the prior written consent of the Company.

---

## 9. NO EQUITY OR PROFIT SHARE

9.1 This Agreement does not create any equity interest, profit-sharing arrangement, or ownership stake in the Company for the Consultant. The Consultant's sole entitlement is the service fees set out in clause 2.1.

---

## 10. TERMINATION & HANDOVER

10.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by **thirty (30) days' written notice**.

10.2 Upon notice of termination, the Consultant shall:
(a) complete all in-progress work within the notice period;
(b) transfer all credentials, access tokens, repository permissions, and documentation to Company-designated accounts within five (5) business days of termination date;
(c) provide up to thirty (30) days of handover support at the standard maintenance rate (₦80,000/month pro-rated) to ensure continuity;
(d) return or securely delete all Company and client Confidential Information from personal devices.

10.3 The Company shall pay all fees for work completed up to the termination date, less any amounts owed for incomplete deliverables.

---

## 11. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

11.2 Disputes shall first be subject to good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within fourteen (14) days of written notice, the matter shall be referred to a single arbitrator under the **Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023**, in Lagos, Nigeria.

12.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions.

12.2 No amendment is valid unless made in writing and signed by both Parties.

12.3 The Consultant may not subcontract obligations under this Agreement without prior written consent.
