## NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

---

## 1. ENGAGEMENT

1.1 The Company engages the Consultant as an independent legal counsel on a non-exclusive, per-deliverable basis. The Consultant shall provide legal services to the Company as requested from time to time in accordance with this Agreement.

1.2 This Agreement does not constitute a contract of employment, partnership, or joint venture. The Consultant shall perform services as an independent contractor and shall be solely responsible for their own tax affairs, except as expressly provided in clause 3.4.

---

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES & RATE CARD

2.1 The Consultant shall provide legal services from the following schedule of services:

| Service | Gross Fee |
|---|---|
| Full client contract bundle (SIA + NDA + any addendum) | ₦100,000 per client |
| Single document — amendment or standalone NDA | ₦35,000 |
| Retainer schedule drafting (e.g., Schedule B to SIA) | Included in bundle fee |
| Legal advisory or dispute instance | ₦50,000 |
| Annual contract review or renewal | ₦25,000 |
| Company registration (CAC filing) | ₦75,000 |

2.2 The Company shall request services in writing (email or WhatsApp message confirming scope). The Consultant shall confirm acceptance and estimated delivery timeframe within two (2) business days.

2.3 Fees may be renegotiated by mutual written agreement. No rate change shall apply to work already in progress.

---

## 3. PAYMENT TERMS

3.1 The Consultant shall submit a written invoice to the Company upon completion of each deliverable.

3.2 Payment shall be made within **five (5) business days of the Company receiving the corresponding client payment** that the deliverable enabled (e.g., for a client contract bundle, payment is triggered by the client's Mobilisation Fee settlement). Where a service is not tied to a client payment milestone, payment shall be made within ten (10) business days of invoice receipt.

3.3 Fees are paid by bank transfer to the Consultant's nominated account. The Company shall not make payments in cash.

3.4 **Withholding Tax:** In compliance with Nigerian tax law, the Company shall deduct Withholding Tax (WHT) at the rate of **five percent (5%)** from each payment and remit the same to the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS). The net amount payable to the Consultant is the gross fee less 5% WHT. The Company shall provide the Consultant with a WHT credit note to support the Consultant's own tax filings.

---

## 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

4.1 All work product created by the Consultant in the course of this engagement — including contract templates, legal documents, draft agreements, and written legal opinions — is the **sole property of the Company** upon creation.

4.2 The Consultant irrevocably assigns to the Company all intellectual property rights in such work product, including copyright. This assignment is effective as of the date each piece of work product is created, and extends to all work performed prior to the execution of this Agreement.

4.3 The Consultant warrants that all work product is original and does not infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party.

---

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY

5.1 The Consultant shall hold all Confidential Information of the Company and its clients in strict confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement for a period of **five (5) years**.

5.2 "**Confidential Information**" includes client identities, commercial terms, software architecture, business strategy, financial data, and all information designated as confidential by the Company.

5.3 The Consultant acknowledges that client information encountered in the course of providing legal services may include patient data and clinically sensitive information. The Consultant shall comply with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023** with respect to any such data.

---

## 6. NON-SOLICITATION

6.1 During the term of this Agreement and for **twelve (12) months** following its termination, the Consultant shall not directly solicit, contract with, or provide services to any client of the Company that was introduced to the Consultant through the Company's engagements, without the prior written consent of the Company.

---

## 7. NO EQUITY OR PROFIT SHARE

7.1 This Agreement does not create any equity interest, profit-sharing arrangement, or ownership stake in the Company for the Consultant. The Consultant's sole entitlement is the service fees set out in clause 2.1.

---

## 8. TERMINATION & HANDOVER

8.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by **thirty (30) days' written notice** to the other Party.

8.2 Upon termination, the Consultant shall: (a) complete any work in progress and deliver all outstanding documents within ten (10) business days; (b) transfer all original files, drafts, and correspondence relating to Company matters; and (c) return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Company, as directed.

8.3 The Company shall pay all fees for work completed up to the termination date. No further fees are payable after that date.

---

## 9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

9.2 Any dispute shall first be subject to good-faith negotiation. If unresolved within fourteen (14) days of written notice, the matter shall be referred to a single arbitrator under the **Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023**, in Lagos, Nigeria.

---

## 10. GENERAL

10.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the Consultant's engagement and supersedes all prior discussions.

10.2 No amendment is valid unless made in writing and signed by both Parties.

10.3 The Consultant may not subcontract or assign obligations under this Agreement without prior written consent of the Company.
